## Findings (written by the same change that committed this record; numbers above are from the klt report)

- **Cost is linear in run length**: 10.33, 10.82, 10.66, 10.79 s per simulated us (20, 50, 100, 200 us) on the fleet worker (`c7i.8xlarge` spot, ngspice, 1 unit per job). Mean about 10.7 s/us. Data volume about 12.8 k samples per us (2.56 M rows, 84 MB of fb.data, at 200 us).
- **Spec window cost**: 8 non-overlapping 100 us windows + 15 us settle = 815 us, extrapolated about 8 700 s (2.4 h) per corner and seed. **That does not fit one fleet job**: the default whole-job ceiling is 3600 s (`batch.max_job_seconds`), which allows at most about 320 us (about 3 windows). A longer unit is killed with its whole job and returns no evidence, so the ladder stopped at 200 us (about 2 160 s) on purpose; 400 and 800 us were not run.
- **Estimator variance versus length** (sigma_W, mean per-window rms of detrended FB edge-time error, relative standard error of the mean): 5 us windows 7.4 % (50 us run, K = 7), 4.9 % (100 us, K = 16), 1.7 % (200 us, K = 36); 12.5 us windows 19.6 % (K = 2), 6.2 % (K = 6), 3.1 % (K = 14); 25 us windows 9.0 % (K = 3), 5.3 % (K = 7). The expected 1/sqrt(K) scaling holds. The 100 us window exists only at 200 us (K = 1): its sigma (0.43 ps) is single-sample and carries no variance. sigma_W grows slowly with W (0.39 to 0.43 ps), as expected for a loop that suppresses low-frequency VCO noise.
- **Convergence at the shortest run (20 us)**: not converged. After the 15 us settle one 5 us window remains (K = 1, no variance) and its sigma (0.67 ps) is about 70 % above the 0.39-0.42 ps seen on longer runs, so the lock transient has not died out: 15 us is too short a settle for this bench at 20 us and the 20 us row should be read as a floor on run length, not an estimate.
- **Divided-down or full 5 GHz? Divided-down applies.** The bench IS the divided-down output (the VCO, prescaler and counters are a behavioural stand-in at f_out/N); the 5 GHz output was not simulated (the pinned VCO does not oscillate, gate G4, and its cost per 5 GHz cycle is unmeasured). This is the spec section 4 fallback: the cross-check runs on the divided-down output with the VCO as a behavioural noise source, and it needs the VCO's measured L(f), which does not exist yet (the placeholder here is the target line).
- **Recommendation for #86 (AC 3)**: the spec window as ONE run per corner does not fit the fleet at the default ceiling. Two ways to make it fit; neither is adopted here (a method change goes through `spec/` with a decision record): (a) 8 independent seeds per corner, each 15 us settle + 100 us window = 115 us, about 1 230 s, 8 units in separate jobs (about 2.7 core-hours per corner; independent-run Welch averaging instead of 8 windows of one run); (b) raise the fleet ceiling to at least 9 000 s for one 815 us unit per corner (a fleet-spec change). Corner count: not the 135-point grid (about 370 core-hours by (a)); start with SLOW / TYP / FAST at nominal supply and temperature = 24 units by (a), about 8 core-hours, and widen only if the spread across those three is not negligible against the 1 ps row-8 limit. The unit timeout must stay under the ceiling minus 120 s.
