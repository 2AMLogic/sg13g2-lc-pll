Supplies, IREF and the drive common mode ramp from 0 V over 3 ns (`loop_power_body.spice`); the window is 40-120 ns. No `.ic`/`.nodeset` seeding of the latches.

- **Owed, not zero**: lock detector, reference and clock buffering have no schematic and are not counted (row 11 allocates up to 4 mW for counters + lock detector and up to 4 mW for buffering).
