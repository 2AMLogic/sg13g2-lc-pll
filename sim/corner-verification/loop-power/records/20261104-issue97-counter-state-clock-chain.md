# Divider counter state and clock chain at TYP 27 C 3.63 V and TYP 125 C 2.97 V (2026-11-04, base `7e98394`, issue #97)

**Result (simulated, not measured): PARTIAL. The cause AC is now supported by measurement: at both corners the CMOS clock the divider flops see is a narrow, skewed pulse (low for only about 0.3-0.4 ns of a 1.6 ns period, and `clkb` peaking at 2.20 V of 2.97 V at 125 C), so the master latch cannot load new data. At 125 C 2.97 V the counter sits in `q = 1111`, `b = 111` with D inputs low and never captures; at 27 C 3.63 V it captures intermittently and counts slowly. No design change is made in this increment, so the fix AC, the 18-corner AC and the power re-report are NOT met.**

## 125 C 2.97 V: the counter is stuck and the flop cannot capture

State, 120-160 ns (state table, thresholded): `q3..q0 = 1000` at 120 ns, then `1111` from 123.2 ns to the last sample (160.0 ns), with `b3..b1 = 111`, `tc = 0`, `tcn = 1`, `s1 s0 = 00` all the way. The flop probe at 140-148 ns shows:

- the D inputs of the four q flops sit at about 0 V (`d0..d3` 0.00 V for most of each period, a 0.5-0.6 V glitch for one 0.4 ns sample), so a working flop would load `0` and leave `1111`;
- `q0` stays 2.97 V; the q0 flop master `m` stays between 2.53 and 2.96 V and never goes low;
- the clock at the flop is a short pulse: `ckl` is above VDD/2 (1.49 V) for most of each 1.6 ns period and low for about 0.35 ns; `clkb` (the inverse, the master-open phase) peaks at **2.20 V (74 % of VDD)** and `ckbb` bottoms at **0.17 V** over the 3.2 ns clock-chain window; at 0.4 ns sampling `clkb` reached only 2.23 V.

So the stuck state is held by the flop, which does not capture, not by the logic: the D inputs ask for a count. This is in steady state at 140-148 ns, not a power-up transient, so it does not point at start-up; whether a nominal start-up changes this was not measured (the nominal-start-up request did not converge).

## 27 C 3.63 V: intermittent capture

State, 120-160 ns: the sequence `q3..q0` is `0111 0110 0101 ... 1011 1010 1001 1000` then `1111` held for 4 clocks (k = 13-16, 140.8-145.6 ns), then `1110 1111 1110 1111 1110 1101 1100 1011 1010` (k = 17-25). A 4-bit down counter run by a clean clock would step `1111 -> 1110 -> 1101`; here it holds `1111` for 4 clocks and alternates `1110` / `1111` for four more, then steps normally. That is consistent with some clock pulses not being captured, which fits the FB period of 44.0 ns against 20 ns seen in `20261104-issue97-modulus-read.md`. It is **not** proven that every missed count is a missed capture, and the samples are at one clock phase (1.6 ns spacing against a 1.601 ns period).

The clock chain at this corner (0.1 ns, 140.0-143.2 ns): `ck` is above VDD/2 for about 1.2 ns of each 1.6 ns period and low for about 0.35 ns; `ckl` is below VDD/2 for about 0.3 ns and its minimum over the window is **0.39 V**; `clkb` peaks at **2.76 V (76 % of VDD)**. `cmo` swings 1.28 to 3.44 V (centre 2.36 V = 65 % of VDD), against 0.97 to 2.57 V (centre 1.77 V = 60 % of VDD) at 125 C 2.97 V.

## What the three TYP failures share

| corner | `cmo` min..max (V) | `ck` low (of 1.6 ns) | `clkb` peak | counter |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TYP 27 C 3.63 V | 1.28..3.44 | ~0.35 ns | 2.76 V of 3.63 V | runs slow (FB 44.0 ns) |
| TYP 125 C 2.97 V | 0.97..2.57 | ~0.5 ns | 2.20 V of 2.97 V | stuck at 1111, no capture |
| TYP 125 C 3.63 V (`20261103-issue97-divider-diag.md`) | min 1.52 | never low (`ck` min 2.94 V) | n/a | clock stays high |

In all three the `cml2cmos` output reaches the flops as a clock whose low phase is short and shallow; at 3.63 V and 125 C the low phase does not exist. **Inference, not measured**: the cmo common mode sits above the switching threshold of `XI1` (an `inv_hv`, threshold not measured here), and more so at higher VDD, so the duty cycle is skewed and the `ckl` buffer chain (one `inv_hv` into three paralleled) turns it into a pulse too narrow for the transmission-gate flop. What is measured is the skewed duty, the sub-rail `clkb` / `ckbb`, and the flop not capturing at 125 C 2.97 V. The `cmo` value is read at the cml2cmos output node of a design whose input is a 5.0 GHz / 8 = 625 MHz prescaler output, so the cause is on the prescaler-to-divider interface, which is why it varies with the prescaler output swing, VDD and temperature together.

## Candidates from the issue

- *Divider timing margin shrinking at high VDD / hot*: **partly supported**: the failing mechanism is clock pulse width / amplitude at the flops, not logic delay (the D inputs are correct at 125 C 2.97 V and the flop simply does not load them).
- *Prescaler output swing / common mode vs the divider input threshold at 3.630 V*: **supported as the direction** (cmo common mode 60-65 % of VDD, swing 1.6-2.2 V), the threshold itself not measured.
- *Start-up*: not the cause at 27 C 3.63 V (steady 44.0 ns period); at 125 C 2.97 V the stuck state is a flop that cannot load in steady state, so a lock-up from start-up is not needed to explain it, but it is **not tested** (nominal-start-up run did not converge).
