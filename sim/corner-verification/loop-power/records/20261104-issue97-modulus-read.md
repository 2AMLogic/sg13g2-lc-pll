# Divider modulus read at TYP 27 C 3.63 V and TYP 125 C 2.97 V (2026-11-04, base `2335505`, issue #97)

**Result (simulated, not measured): PARTIAL. Step 1 of the remaining increment is done: the two "FB is not 50 MHz" corners are two different failures, neither is a bench start-up artefact, and the clock reaching the divider is correct at both. The cause inside the divider is NOT yet traced, no design change is made, and the issue's fix and 18-corner ACs are NOT met.**

- **Method**: four single-corner `klt sim` requests on the batch fleet, `timeout_s` 1800, stage-2 body and ramped start-up unchanged, `.meas` only (no waveform files pulled; `artifacts.raw` was null).
  - 400 ns transient (`4p 400n 0 4p`): `request-modulus-97-a.json` (27 C 3.63 V, job `klt-sim-92de66363758`, 574 s) and `-b.json` (125 C 2.97 V, job `klt-sim-7fca2195e377`, 555 s). Rising-edge times (1.65 V) of `fb` and of `xdiv.tc` (mcounter terminal count), from td = 40 ns; max/min of `xdiv.xm.q0..q3` over 40-400 ns.
  - 200 ns transient: `request-modulus-97-a2.json` (job `klt-sim-b2a5a29aaf29`, 278 s) and `-b2.json` (job `klt-sim-b9b1d12711c6`, 292 s). Time for 10 rising edges of `xdiv.ck` (cml2cmos output) and `xdiv.ckl` (buffered divider clock) from td = 100 ns, their max/min over 100-200 ns, max/min of `xdiv.tc` and `fb` over 100-200 ns, TC rise/fall times. (The 100-edge `tck100`/`tckl100` meas are unreached by construction: 100 edges at 1.6 ns do not fit in 100 ns. Not a finding.)
- **Simulator**: ngspice-46 on the fleet against the pin of 47 (`sim/pins.json`): **the pin is not met.** The dispatch host (ngspice-42) cannot load the pinned `psp103.osdi`; nothing was run locally. Each corner was run once per request; none was repeated, so run-to-run repeatability is not shown.
- **Expected ratio**: the prescaler output is 5.0 GHz / 8 = 625 MHz (clock period 1.6 ns). N = 100 means 12 clocks of 1.6 ns plus the swallowed clocks, FB period 20 ns (`design/README.md`, `scripts/divider_counts.py`: N = 8 x clocks + 2 x MC-high clocks).

- **Read (27 C 3.63 V)**: the clock is present at the right rate and full swing at both `ck` and `ckl`, the counter runs (TC and FB toggle at one regular 44.0 ns period), and TC and FB are in step. So the counter divides, but by about 2.2x too much: 44.0 ns is about 27 clocks of 1.6 ns, against 12 (+ swallow) expected. With the 2 swallowed clocks lengthening to 1.8 ns this is roughly 25 clocks. The cause is **not traced**: whether the mcounter reloads late, skips its terminal count (a second pass through the count), or the acounter/MC path misbehaves at this VDD was not measured. This **replaces** the earlier "FB toggles" note with an actual period and rules out a missing or distorted clock as the cause at this corner.
- **Read (125 C 2.97 V)**: the clock is present at the right rate, so the prescaler and `cml2cmos` pass edges (though `ckl` bottoms at 0.32 V in the late window, against about -0.03 V at 27 C: a weaker low level, noted and **not** shown to be the cause). TC and FB go low once (TC at 78.5 ns, FB rising at 72.8 ns) and stay low for the rest of the 400 ns run, while the q flops swing rail to rail over the whole window (the q min/max cover the start-up, so they do not show the state after 100 ns). That fits a counter that has entered a state it does not leave, or a TC that is never set again. Not measured: the q and b bit states late in the run.
- **Different from TYP 125 C 3.63 V**, where `ck` never goes low (`20261103-issue97-divider-diag.md`): there the cause is the cml2cmos output level. Here and at 27 C 3.63 V the clock is good, so these two are counter-side failures. Three mechanisms across the four TYP corners, all converged, none from non-convergence.
- **Start-up is not the cause of the 27 C 3.63 V result**: the FB period is steady at 44.0 ns from the second interval to 395 ns, which a power-up transient would not do. Still not refuted for 125 C 2.97 V, where a lock-up state could be entered during start-up; no nominal-start-up comparison was run at this corner.
- **Not done / owed**: (1) read the mcounter and acounter state per clock at both corners (needs the `q0..q3`, `b1..b3`, `s0`, `s1` and `MC` edge times, or a waveform pull, over a window of a few FB periods) to say where the 44.0 ns period and the 125 C hang come from; (2) the design fix for `cml2cmos` (TYP 125 C 3.63 V) and for whatever (1) points at, confined to the adopted divider/prescaler cells; (3) the 18-corner stage-2 grid via `klt sim` on the fleet and its per-corner table; (4) the nominal row-11 power re-report against #84. Nothing about power changed in this increment (`ipavg` -1.95 mA, `idavg` -4.3 mA at 27 C 3.63 V over 100-200 ns, a window mean of a non-dividing divider, not a row-11 number).
