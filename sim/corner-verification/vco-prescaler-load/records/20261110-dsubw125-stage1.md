## 27 C control: same substitution vs the unmodified 27 C result

- 9 of 9 27 C units have an unmodified counterpart in `20261110-stage1-corners.csv` and converged under both. Modified minus unmodified: f +3.2 to +3.8 MHz (max 0.09 % of f), Vpp +1.60 to +1.72 mV (max 0.28 %), startup proxy -0.20 to -0.14 ps.
- **The control is close but not identical**: the substitution moves every 27 C figure in the same direction (the diode's `tnom` also rescales its junction parameters, not only the leakage), by the amounts above. The unmodified values in the CSV carry 4 significant figures, so the offset is above their rounding.

## Leakage bound for the approximation

- One `dsubw` instance, reverse 1.65 V, 125 C: 5.09e-10 A with `tnom = 27`, 1.66e-12 A with `tnom = 125` (2AMLogic/sg13g2-vco#303, single-point ngspice-46 probe). Difference over the 32 varactor instances: at most (5.09e-10 - 1.66e-12) x 32 = **16.2 nA total**.
- Supply current printed in the 125 C `loaded` units' `ngspice.log` (`vsup#branch`, operating-point value, same for every Vctrl at one Vdd): 1880 to 2059 uA; the bound is 7.9e-06 to 8.6e-06 of it.
- The ruling quoted "mA" tank bias; the unloaded VCO's printed supply current is ~0.1 mA (the loaded case adds the prescaler's ~2 mA). The bound stays small either way; it bounds the **leakage** difference only, not the other effect of the card's `tnom` (see the 27 C control).

## Gate G2 at 125 C (this stage)

- loaded: 17 of 45 125 C units converged under the approximation; 0 of those are below 0.40 V (row 7) and 0 never reach the 0.2 V startup threshold. Swing and startup are therefore graded for the converged units only, labelled approximation (`dsubw` TNOM = 125 C); 28 unit(s) remain `error` (ungraded).
- **G2 is NOT recorded as closed for 125 C by this stage**: closure under the label needs every 125 C unit graded and row 7 met, and neither holds here. Row 1 (4.5-5.5 GHz) is out of scope (#173) and also unmet at every converged unit. A "physical failure" verdict on https://github.com/2AMLogic/sg13g2-vco/issues/309 would also reopen it.
- Units that still abort do so at the same place as the unmodified run (`Timestep too small; initial timepoint: trouble with xq1`, see `ngspice.log` of each unit); the substitution cured the `dsubw` trigger of the unloaded VCO (all 18 unloaded 125 C units of stage 2 converge) but not every loaded unit. Cause of the remainder: **not traced** (no other card, deck or VCO change was allowed). No tool gap established; nothing filed on `2AMLogic/klayout-tools`.
