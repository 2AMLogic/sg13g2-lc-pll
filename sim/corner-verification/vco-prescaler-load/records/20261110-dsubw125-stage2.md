## Leakage bound for the approximation

- One `dsubw` instance, reverse 1.65 V, 125 C: 5.09e-10 A with `tnom = 27`, 1.66e-12 A with `tnom = 125` (2AMLogic/sg13g2-vco#303, single-point ngspice-46 probe). Difference over the 32 varactor instances: at most (5.09e-10 - 1.66e-12) x 32 = **16.2 nA total**.
- Supply current printed in the 125 C `loaded` units' `ngspice.log` (`vsup#branch`, operating-point value, same for every Vctrl at one Vdd): 1857 to 2096 uA; the bound is 7.7e-06 to 8.7e-06 of it.
- Supply current printed in the 125 C `unloaded` units' `ngspice.log` (`vsup#branch`, operating-point value, same for every Vctrl at one Vdd): 93 to 120 uA; the bound is 1.3e-04 to 1.7e-04 of it.
- The ruling quoted "mA" tank bias; the unloaded VCO's printed supply current is ~0.1 mA (the loaded case adds the prescaler's ~2 mA). The bound stays small either way; it bounds the **leakage** difference only, not the other effect of the card's `tnom` (see the 27 C control).
