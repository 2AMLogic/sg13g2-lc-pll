# cp-dc stage 2 re-run after the XBPC retune (20261103-221e27b)

**Result (simulated, not measured): all 18 stage-2 sub-corners pass spec row 7 at V_out = 1.65 V; worst up/down mismatch 1.57 % of I_cp (SLOW / 2.97 V / 125 C), down from 2.19 % at FAST / 3.63 V / -40 C. Stage 1 (15 points, 3.30 V) also passes, worst 0.95 %.**

- **Change**: `design/cp_bias.sch` XBPC (PMOS cascode-bias diode) w 3u -> 4u, l 1u unchanged; `design/netlist/cp.spice` regenerated with `design/netlist.sh cp` (`klt netlist`). No other device touched. Commit `221e27b`.
- **Why**: the 3u diode set the source-leg cascode bias at a lower current density than the leg's (20 uA / 3u vs 190 uA / 38u at K = 9.5), so the leg mirror device saw more Vds than its reference and I_up ran high at high supply. 4u is the leg density at K = 9.5. Mechanism confirmed by single-corner local probes (3u: 2.19 %, 3.5u: 1.77 %, 4u: 1.36 % at FAST / 3.63 V / -40 C); not separately traced beyond that.
- **Method**: `klt sim` on the batch fleet (`backend batch`), requests `request-stage1.json` and `request-stage2.json` unchanged. Stage 2 job `klt-sim-2e00c4ef0e97`, stage 1 job `klt-sim-edf18b3bafa5`. Reports: `20261103-221e27b-stage{1,2}.json`.
- **Simulator**: ngspice-46 on the fleet against sim/pins.json pin 47 (mismatch, same as the prior records).
- **Neighbours of the failing corner** (mm_165, before -> after): FAST / 3.63 V / -40 C 2.19 -> 1.36 %; FAST / 3.63 V / 27 C 1.99 -> 1.12 %; TYP / 3.63 V / -40 C 1.71 -> 0.90 %. Other corners are in the JSON; the retune moved mismatch from the high-supply to the low-supply corners: SLOW / 2.97 V / -40 C 0.68 -> 1.55 %, SLOW / 2.97 V / 125 C 0.60 -> 1.57 %. Margin to the 2 % bound is now >= 0.43 pt everywhere.
- **Trade-off, disclosed**: `vtop_est` (the contiguous <= 2 % window above 1.65 V, informational) fell by 0.05 to 0.10 V at most corners (e.g. SLOW / 2.97 V / 125 C 1.90 -> 1.80 V; FAST / 3.63 V / -40 C unchanged at 2.9 V). The V_hi,req edge stays gated by G4 and is not claimed here; if V_hi,req lands above the new window edge at a low-supply corner, the retune must be revisited.
- **Other CP-dependent benches re-run**: `sim/cp-dc/records/20261103-030519-221e27b-icp-corners.md` (I_cp 189.40 to 203.87 uA, all converged) and `sim/loop-model/records/20261103-030519-221e27b-row5-envelope.md` (row 5 still PASS, f_c 0.777 to 1.248 MHz, PM min 52.48 deg).
