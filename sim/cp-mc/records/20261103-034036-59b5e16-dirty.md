# cp-mc run 20261103-034036-59b5e16-dirty

**Result (simulated, not measured; Monte Carlo of per-instance device mismatch only).** Pooled over the 33-corner grid at 200 samples per corner, `klt yield` status against the row-7 / row-9 **target** limits is `fail`; per-measurement numbers are in the tables. **Spec row 7 is NOT closed:** V_hi,req is unresolved (gate G4), so the upper compliance edge is not pinned; the V_out points below are probes, not the requirement. **Spec row 9 is NOT closed:** only the CP-mismatch to reference-ripple contribution is covered here, not the locked-loop spur (method T).

- **Method**: `klt sim` Monte Carlo (`vary: mismatch`, seed 20261103, n = 200 per corner, 33 corners), `ngspice` `op` steps on ONE instance of `cp` (design/netlist/cp.spice, drift-checked against design/cp.sch), per sample UP-on/DN-off then DN-on/UP-off at each V_out, so I_up and I_dn share one mismatch draw. mismatch % = |I_up - I_dn| / 200 uA x 100 (static DC; no switching, charge sharing or pulse-width effect). Ripple vr = 2 |I_up - I_dn| (t_rst/T_ref = 0.1, a stated DR-001 allowance, not derived) |Z_filter(50 MHz)| (83.10 ohm from sim/loop-model/loop-filter.json, unloaded R3-C3 pole included): first-harmonic ripple from the mismatch only. Spur in dBc is not computed here.
- **Estimator**: `klt yield` (klt 0.8.1.dev1184+gc587a95f7771), Clopper-Pearson empirical yield, 95 % confidence; limits `sim/cp-mc/limits-target.json` (row 7: 2 %; row 9: 0.52 mV peak) and `limits-fallback.json` (6 %; -40 dBc = 1.644 mV). `target_yield` 0.9973 (3 sigma) is **this campaign's choice; the ratified spec states no yield target.**
- **Pooling caveat**: the estimate pools all 33 corners with equal weight, a worst-case envelope and not one distribution (klt yield warns of this); the per-corner table gives the worst single corner.
- **Corners**: MOS {tt,ss,ff,sf,fs} x T {-40,27,125} C at 3.300 V (15) + {SLOW=ss, TYP=tt, FAST=ff} x {2.970, 3.630} V x T (18) = 33; every point uses the `mos_*_mismatch` section of cornerMOShv.lib. The CP has no MIM or HBT, so those stage-1 axes do not apply. Stage 3 (405-point cross) not run.
- **Negative control**: same grid and seed, n = 20 per corner, up-leg mirror+cascode widened 3.8u->4.6u (about +20 % I_up; the skew of scripts/test-cp-dc-negative.sh); `klt yield-samples` derives the control from the two unedited reports.
- **Backend**: batch fleet. sharded into batch jobs of at most 696 units (job ids in the `klt sim` stderr files beside this record; the fleet launched them over several `--resume` passes because of capacity limits); engine ngspice-46 on `worker`. Local ngspice is 46 and the repo pins 47 (sim/pins.json): the pin check in scripts/lib-pdk.sh warns on this host, and a 2-sample batch probe reproduced the local values bit for bit.
- **PDK**: IHP-Open-PDK v0.3.0, libraries staged to the worker (`options.stage_model_inputs`); psp103.osdi from the pinned PDK. Note sim/pins.json hashes cornerMOShv.lib but not its `sg13g2_moshv_*mismatch.lib` includes; the staged-input sha256s are in the nominal report's `environment.staged_model_inputs`.
