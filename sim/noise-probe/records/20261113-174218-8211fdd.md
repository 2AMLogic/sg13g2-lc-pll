## (a) Units calibration

A 1 kohm resistor read through a noiseless unity VCVS, so the output noise is the
resistor's own.

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| `onoise_spectrum` at 1 kHz and 10 kHz | 4.071372e-09 |
| sqrt(4kTR) at R = 1 kohm, T = 300.15 K | 4.0714e-09 |
| 4kTR at the same R, T | 1.6576e-17 |

**`onoise_spectrum` is an amplitude density in V/sqrt(Hz), not a power density in V^2/Hz.**
Every number below is therefore an amplitude density, and a power spectral density S is its
square. This matters: reading the vector as V^2/Hz would overstate the charge pump's noise
power by about 11 orders of magnitude.

## (b) Charge pump: output current noise, both legs steered to VOUT

Operating point: UP and DN both asserted (the UP/DN reset-overlap state in lock), output
held at Vctrl = 2.0 V by an ideal source, IREF = 20 uA.

**DC check, with the per-leg currents measured in this same deck and run** (three further
copies of `cp` at the same netlist, corner, forced Vctrl = 2.0 V and IREF = 20 uA, each
with its own noiseless 0 V sense; sign convention is current *into* the output node, so the
sourcing leg reads positive and the sinking leg negative):

| UP / DN | sense | i (A) | reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 / 1 | `i(VSENSE)` | -2.08029e-06 | the probed point: both tails steered to VOUT |
| 1 / 0 | `i(VSENSEU)` | 1.962727e-04 | I_up = 196.273 uA |
| 0 / 1 | `i(VSENSED)` | -1.98353e-04 | I_dn = 198.353 uA |
| 0 / 0 | `i(VSENSEZ)` | -4.01879e-15 | both tails dumped to VDUMP |

I_up - I_dn = 196.273 - 198.353 = **-2.080 uA**, which reproduces the probed point's
`i(VSENSE) = -2.08029e-06` A to every digit carried. So the 2.1 uA residual is the
*difference of two ~0.2 mA legs that are both live at VOUT*, not a quiet node: the pump is
in the balanced steering state, which is the state DR-007 defines method N's operating
point as.

The 0/0 row is why the residual alone would not have shown this. Both tails dumped also
reads a small current -- 4 fA -- so "small against a leg" is satisfied by a state in which
*no* leg reaches the output, and by a deck with UP/DN polarity reversed. The per-leg
figures distinguish them; the residual on its own does not. DR-007 section 5 field 3
therefore requires both.

These per-leg currents are DC-only copies with no `ac` drive, sharing only the ideal VDD
and ground nodes with the copy under test, and the 40 numeric noise rows below are
byte-identical to those of the 17:14:43 run of the deck without them, so adding them did
not perturb the noise analysis.

| 3.1623e+05 | 2.529593e-11 | 6.3988e-22 |
| 1.0000e+06 | 1.793199e-11 | 3.2156e-22 |
| 3.1623e+06 | 1.483844e-11 | 2.2018e-22 |
| 1.0000e+07 | 1.337807e-11 | 1.7897e-22 |

(The full 31-point decade-10 sweep from 10 kHz to 10 MHz is in the ngspice log.)

The shape is a 1/f region below roughly 300 kHz falling onto a near-white floor that
has reached 1.338e-11 A/sqrt(Hz) at 10 MHz and is still drifting down there. Between 10 kHz and 100 kHz the amplitude falls by a factor 3.11 for a decade in
frequency, against sqrt(10) = 3.16 for a pure 1/f power law -- flicker-dominated, as
expected for the long-channel HV MOS tail and cascode devices at 200 uA.

**Integrated**: integral of S_i df from 10 kHz to (pi/2) x 1 MHz = 1.5708 MHz (DR-001's
equivalent brick-wall in-band noise bandwidth), trapezoid on linear f over the 31-point
grid, log-log interpolation at the upper limit: **1.0424e-15 A^2**. DR-007 "Illustration"
uses this figure; it is not a method-N result, because the on-time it must be gated by is
not stated anywhere yet (that is DR-007's point).

## (c) npn13G2v: collector current noise

Common emitter, VBE = 0.85 V, collector held at 3.3 V through the same noiseless current
sense. DC check: `i(VSENSEQ) = 1.160036e-03` A.

| f (Hz) | i_n (A/sqrt(Hz)) | S_i (A^2/Hz) |
|---|---|---|
| 1.0000e+04 | 2.114419e-11 | 4.4708e-22 |
| 1.0000e+05 | 1.643523e-11 | 2.7012e-22 |
| 1.0000e+06 | 1.590920e-11 | 2.5310e-22 |
| 1.0000e+07 | 1.743197e-11 | 3.0387e-22 |

Non-zero, with a 1/f corner near 30 kHz and a white plateau from about 100 kHz. Full
collector shot noise at I_C = 1.160 mA would be 2qI_C = 3.717e-22 A^2/Hz, i.e. 1.928e-11
A/sqrt(Hz); the 1 MHz plateau reads 1.591e-11, 83 % of that in amplitude. The right order
with the right sign of departure (the base-resistance and base-shot terms seen through the
0 V base drive do not add the full uncorrelated shot contribution), so VBIC is returning
device noise rather than a default or a zero.

## What this probe does and does not settle

**Settles**: that `.noise` runs to completion and returns non-zero, physically-shaped
device noise for both model families, at the nominal corner. DR-007 relies on exactly that
and on nothing else from this record.

**Does not settle**: whether gating a flicker-dominated spectrum by a periodic switch is
well approximated by scaling its PSD by the on-time fraction (it is not, in general -- see
DR-007 "Limits"); anything at any other corner; anything about the PFD or the divider, both
of which DR-007 takes *out* of method N; and any value of the charge pump's on-time in
lock, which this probe does not measure.
