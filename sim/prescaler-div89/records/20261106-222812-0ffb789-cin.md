# prescaler-div89 first-stage input admittance 20261106-222812-0ffb789

**Result (simulated, nominal corner only, not measured): differential input capacitance of the P = 8/9 prescaler at the tank-node pins is 1.837 fF at 5.0 GHz and 1.950 fF at 5.5 GHz (shunt conductance -34.9 uS and -38.5 uS), against the 2.1 fF device-plus-routing budget of spec section 1: the device-level figure is under the budget with 0.26 fF (5.0 GHz) and 0.15 fF (5.5 GHz) left for routing. The pre-change /8-/10 prescaler measured 1.373 fF and 1.460 fF with the same deck, so the new stage is +0.46 fF (+34 %) on the tank. Corners are NOT covered, and the VCO swing/startup re-check with this load is NOT done: the claim "the loop works" stays withheld.**

- **Why it rose**: the input device is unchanged (the same `cml_ef` pair, one `npn13G2v` Nx = 1 base per side). Its emitter followers now drive four latch clock pairs (`cml_div23`: two `cml_dff`) instead of two (`div2_cml`), which raises the base-side capacitance seen through the follower. This reading is from the netlists, not from a separate experiment.
- **Method**: `sim/prescaler-div89/cin_probe.spice.tmpl`, one local `ngspice -b` per netlist: `op`, then AC 1 V differential (0.5 V each side) at 5.0 and 5.5 GHz at the DC operating point of the unclocked prescaler (MC low, ideal IREF = 50 uA, `hbt_typ`, 27 C, 3.3 V); C = Im(Yin)/(2 pi f). No transient cross-check of the fundamental (the G1 bench did one).
