## 4. The headline: a pin bump cannot move `f_osc`

The C-grid records in `sim/corner-verification/vco-prescaler-load/records/` show the pinned VCO at
3.670–4.318 GHz with **0 of 36** stage-2 corners inside the 4.5–5.5 GHz band (row 1), and 0 of 90 at
stage 1. **None of that is pin staleness.** Those decks are built from the fetched
`design/vco.spice` and `sim/inductor-model/sg13g2_inductor_em.spice`, and both files are
**byte-identical** at `17e49a8` and `49a0439a` (§1). The simulation input would not change by one
byte, so bumping the pin to `49a0439a` cannot change a single simulated frequency, amplitude or
startup-margin number, and re-running the grid on the new pin would be a null experiment.

Stated the other way: **G2 is a VCO design limit on this pin and on upstream `main` alike, not a
stale-reference artifact.** Closing it needs a tank or varactor change in `sg13g2-vco` (an issue on
that repository, per this repo's scope discipline), not a bump here. No open upstream issue proposes
one — the only open frequency-adjacent item is `sg13g2-vco#198` (bounded bn=substrate varactor
characterization per DR-006, gated on ratification), which is a characterization task, not a resize.

The one upside a bump *would* bring is in layout, §5.

## 5. What a bump would bring, and what it would invalidate

### What it would bring: a non-forbidden access layer for VCTRL

`layout/README.md` records 3 keep-out violations, *all* the VCTRL pin-access stack: Metal3/4/5 at
the VCO's Metal3-only VCTRL pin, about 57 µm inside the bbox, with `sg13g2-vco#314` named as the
requested VCO change and `klayout-tools#5198` as the tool gap. `layout/gen_top.py`'s
`FORBIDDEN_LAYERS` is Activ, GatPoly, Cont, pSD, Metal1–Metal5 and their fillers — **TopMetal1 (126)
and TopMetal2 (134) are not in it**, which is why the tank routes on TopMetal1 are already outside
the forbidden set. A VCTRL route that terminates on the new TopMetal2 pin would therefore need no
forbidden-layer shape inside the keep-out at all, and the Metal3/4/5 stack that produces all three
intrusions would no longer be required.

Both blockers that `layout/README.md` cites are now closed: `sg13g2-vco#314` (`COMPLETED`, 2026-11-10) and
`klayout-tools#5198` — *"keepout-check: allow pin-access windows; no verb to route between placed
cells' labelled pins"* — closed 2026-11-10T07:38:53Z.

**This is an analysis, not a result.** It follows from the forbidden-layer set and the pin's layer
and position; it is **not** a `klt keepout-check` run, which is out of scope here. Confirming it
requires bumping the pin, re-pointing `layout/gen_integrated.py`'s VCTRL route at the TopMetal2 pin
(it currently targets `("vco", "VCTRL")`, resolved to the Metal3 label), regenerating
`layout/top.gds`, and re-running the check. Until then, "3 → 0" is the expected outcome, not a
recorded one.

## 7. Recommendation

**Bump for the layout, with eyes open; do not bump expecting a frequency change.**

The bump's entire value is the TopMetal2 VCTRL pin, which plausibly clears all three recorded
keep-out violations and retires the `sg13g2-vco#314` / `klayout-tools#5198` pair that
`layout/README.md` currently names as the blockers — both now closed. Its cost is regenerating
`layout/top.gds` and re-issuing every artifact in §5 A: the item 2 attestation, the item 3 DRC
report and cross-check, the provenance record, the three layout READMEs, and `scripts/test-layout-top.sh`'s
intrusion-count assertion plus its negative control. Items 2 and 3 are currently `met`; they go
stale the moment the pin moves and stay stale until that work lands, and the signoff CI compares the
committed record against a fresh `klt signoff` run on every push. The bump is therefore not a
one-line edit to `reuse.lock.json` — it is a layout regeneration with a DRC re-run behind it, and it
should be scheduled as one.

It buys nothing for G2. The simulation inputs are byte-identical, so no recorded frequency,
amplitude or startup number can move, and no corner grid is worth re-running on the new pin. G2 needs
a tank or varactor change in `sg13g2-vco`, which is that repository's decision and this repository's
interface specification — not a reference update here.

Suggested order: close the layout work (regenerate, re-run DRC, re-stamp item 2 and item 3, re-base
the keep-out assertions) in one change, so the ladder never sits with items 2 and 3 stale against a
bumped pin. Nothing in this record justifies touching `spec/`, and nothing in it is a change request
against `sg13g2-vco`.
