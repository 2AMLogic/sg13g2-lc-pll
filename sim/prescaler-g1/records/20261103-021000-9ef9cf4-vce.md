# prescaler-vce run 20261103-021000-9ef9cf4

**Result (simulated, not measured): worst HBT |Vce| against BVCEO(min) 2.2 V: **FAIL**. At the minimum supply 2.970 V the worst converged corner is 2.340 V (SLOW/novdd/-40C/params:vddv=2.97, x1_x45_xmc_xqm1); at 3.630 V it is 2.858 V (FAST/novdd/27C/params:vddv=3.63, x1_x2_xef_xqf2). 14 of 18 corners exceed the rating; 4 did not converge and are not graded.**

- **Scope**: the 18 supply sub-corners of spec section 'Corner set' stage 2: {2.970, 3.630} V x {SLOW, TYP, FAST} x {-40, 27, 125} C, SLOW = hbt_wcs, TYP = hbt_typ, FAST = hbt_bcs (the prescaler has HBTs and ideal resistors only, so the MOS and MIM members of the bundles do not enter). The 3.630 V half is beyond the issue's 2.970 V ask: Vce stress rises with the supply, so it is the corner a rating check has to include. Not covered: the 135-point 3.3 V grid, Monte Carlo, routing parasitics, the VCO driving the input (gate G2), self-heating beyond the model's.
- **Method**: `klt sim` corner request `prescaler_vce.request.json` (generated by `scripts/gen_prescaler_vce_request.py` from `design/netlist/prescaler.spice`, one measurement per HBT, 95 devices). Transient `1p 20n 10n 2p`: data are recorded from 10 ns (the dividers have settled), 50 input cycles; each measurement is `vecmax(abs(v(collector)-v(emitter)))` over 10-20 ns, so a transient peak counts, not just the DC level. Stimulus as `prescaler_g1.spice.tmpl`: 0.4 Vpp differential 5.0 GHz at a common mode equal to the supply, IREF = 50 uA, MC = 0 (/4 mode), ideal resistors.
- **Limit**: BVCEO 2.2 V min (2.5 V typ), the figure from the #44 EE key review, applied to the instantaneous |Vce| of every HBT. BVCEO is an open-base rating; with the base driven the device tolerates more, so a transient excursion over it is a flag for the designer, not a proven breakdown. The record reports it as the issue asks: against 2.2 V.
- **Simulator**: `ngspice-46` on the Spot batch fleet (`environment.engine_host` = worker), against sim/pins.json pin 47: **mismatch (fleet ran 46)**. Job `klt-sim-a11463a45046`, m7i.4xlarge, spot.
