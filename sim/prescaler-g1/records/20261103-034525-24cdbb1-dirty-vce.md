Transient `1p 20n 10n 2p`: data are recorded from 10 ns (the dividers have settled), 50 input cycles; each measurement is `vecmax(abs(v(collector)-v(emitter)))` over 10-20 ns, so a transient peak counts, not just the DC level. Stimulus as `prescaler_g1.spice.tmpl`: 0.4 Vpp differential 5.0 GHz at a common mode equal to the supply, IREF = 50 uA, MC = 0 (/4 mode), ideal resistors.
- **Limit**: BVCEO 2.2 V min (2.5 V typ), the figure from the #44 EE key review, applied to the instantaneous |Vce| of every HBT. BVCEO is an open-base rating; with the base driven the device tolerates more, so a transient excursion over it is a flag for the designer, not a proven breakdown. The record reports it as the issue asks: against 2.2 V.
